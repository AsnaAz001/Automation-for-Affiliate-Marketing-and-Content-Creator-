# 💸 Automated Affiliate Marketing & Multi-Platform Content Pipeline

An end-to-end affiliate marketing automation system built with **Make.com**. This pipeline automates product data extraction, AI-driven copywriting, SEO blog publishing, and multi-channel social media distribution without manual effort.

## 🎯 Problem Solved
Managing affiliate marketing across multiple platforms requires daily manual effort—writing product reviews, formatting blog posts, creating social media captions, and uploading promotional videos. This system reduces hours of daily manual work into a fully automated, scheduled pipeline.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Integrations
* **Database & Trigger:** Google Sheets (Stores product details, affiliate links, and media references; triggers daily at 15:00)
* **Generative AI:** Google Gemini AI (Generates SEO-friendly blog articles, engaging social captions, and video descriptions tailored to each platform)
* **Blogging & SEO:** Blogger (Automatically formats and publishes daily product review posts with embedded affiliate links)
* **Social Media Distribution:**
  * **Instagram for Business:** Auto-publishes promotional product posts
  * **Pinterest:** Creates and pins visual product cards for organic traffic
  * **Facebook Pages:** Cross-posts product updates to reach wider audiences
* **Video Automation:** Google Drive & YouTube (Fetches product videos from Drive and auto-uploads them to YouTube with AI-generated metadata)

---

## ⚙️ Key Highlights
* **Zero Manual Intervention:** Runs autonomously once every 24 hours.
* **Platform-Specific Optimization:** Uses separate AI prompts to ensure content fits the tone of blogs, pins, and short-form social posts.
* **Scalable Design:** Easily adaptable to any e-commerce or affiliate niche by simply updating the central Google Sheet.
